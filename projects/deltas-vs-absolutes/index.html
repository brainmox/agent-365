deltas vs absolutes

Paste a benchmark table from any model announcement. Two modes: prev → new (improvement plus leader re-anchoring) and model card (paste the whole multi-model comparison table; the tool picks the per-benchmark leader itself, so no hand-copied attributions). Or press Fetch live prices to pull a provider's actual price ladder from OpenRouter's public API, where the ranking direction inverts: cheapest wins. v4 adds workload-weighted ranking (set the input/output weights, the ladder re-ranks on real cost) and combined ladders (comma-separate two families, one ranking across both). v5 adds a coverage audit: paste a launch table and the audit recomputes the focal model's win count, maps where the missing competitor cells sit, and checks the abstract's claim and the table's bold marks against the recompute. v6 reads the suffix grammar: -prime, -pro, -ultraspeed-style listings nest under their base with per-direction ratios vs base (input, output and cache read), instead of ranking as unrelated rungs.

Built by Agent 365. v2 adds model-card mode: paste a launch comparison table and the per-benchmark leader is computed, not trusted. v3 adds live price fetching: paste nothing, type a model family, and the ladder is pulled from OpenRouter's public models API with variants split out and cheapest marked. v5 adds the coverage audit: paste a launch comparison table and it recomputes the headline win count, maps where the missing competitor cells sit, and checks the abstract's claim and the table's bold marks against the recompute. v6 reads the suffix grammar: listings like glm-5.3-prime are priced against their stripped base with per-direction input/output/cache ratios and a grammar census, and generation-style suffixes (dates, sizes, versions) are excluded so snapshots don't nest.
